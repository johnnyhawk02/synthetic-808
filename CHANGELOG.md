# Changelog

The version is shown next to the title on the page and in `package.json`.

## 0.2.1

- Hand clap refitted: the separate claps were smeared together (the gaps between them only
  fell about 20 dB, against about 30 dB on a real 808). The claps are now distinct, the noise
  band is narrower and the reverb tail darker, at the same loudness.

## 0.2.0

- Bass drum, toms and congas, rim shot and claves, hand clap and maracas, cowbell and hi-hats
  rebuilt along the lines of the TR-808 circuits.
- New controls: bass drum Click and Attack, snare Wires, cowbell Ring, Spread, open and closed
  hat Tone. Knobs that do nothing in the alternate modes are hidden.
- Snare Fit / Circuit switch: Circuit plays the snare built from the circuit; Fit (the default)
  is the snare refitted to the recordings.

## 0.1.0

- First release: the twelve voices, sequencer page, phone layout and the 808 sample set.
