Synthetic 808 v0.2.1

Twelve drum voices, synthesised sample by sample in your browser

Knobs go past their standard range for tuning; the orange arc on each dial marks the 0 to 1 span. Drag, scroll or use the arrow keys, hold Shift for fine moves, double-click for the default. Drag up or down, double-tap for the default. A dot marks knobs moved from the default. The 808 chip on each voice swaps it for a recording of a real TR-808, picked by its nearest knob positions.

Pattern

Tap a step to add a hit, again to accent it, once more to clear. Pads play from the keyboard: A–', hold Shift to accent, Space starts and stops.

808 samples: Michael Fischer's free TR-808 sample set (1994).

Sound engine: synthetic-808, plain ES modules running in an AudioWorklet.
